Cloud Save Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains how Cloud Save Manager, a personal desktop app by Uzair Sohail, handles Google user data.

Data the app accesses

With your permission, the app uses the Google Drive drive.file scope. This lets it create, read, update and delete files in Google Drive that it created or that you opened with it. It can't browse the rest of your Drive.

The files it handles are your game save files, versions of those files, and related information such as file names, checksums, timestamps and device names.

How the data is used

The app uses this data only to back up your game saves, keep them in sync across your devices, and restore earlier versions. Google user data isn't used for advertising or sold.

Where the data is stored

Your save files are stored on your own devices and in your own Google Drive. The app connects directly from your device to Google. The developer doesn't run a server for the app and doesn't receive your files or Google data.

Sign-in tokens and app settings are stored on your device.

Sharing

The app doesn't share Google user data with anyone. Google processes your data under its own terms and privacy policy when you use Google services.

Limited Use

Cloud Save Manager's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

You can remove the app's access at any time in your Google Account connections. To delete stored saves, delete the app's folder from Google Drive and remove the app's folder from your devices.

Changes and contact

Any changes to this policy will be published on this page. For questions, open an issue on GitHub.
